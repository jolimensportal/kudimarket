## 2. Why the domain is not evidence of anything

We confirmed `kudimarket.com` is unregistered via WHOIS and RDAP. **That proves
nothing about trademark rights.** It tells us nobody has claimed the string, which
can also mean nobody has bothered to register it — or that someone registered the
mark and abandoned the domain.

**A CAC company-name search does not cover trademarks.** We have confirmed this
against published Nigerian IP due-diligence guidance, which states explicitly that
the CAC name-availability search does not include the trademark, copyright or
patent databases, and that a successful CAC registration confers no IP rights.

This is a search, not a filing. We want to know what exists before we commit.

## 4. What we need you to search

For **each** class in section 3:

1. **Exact** — `KUDI MARKET`, `KUDIMARKET`
2. **Phonetic** — approximate matches on the spoken form ("koodi market", "kudi market")
3. **Visual** — look-alike word marks in the same classes
4. **Component** — `KUDI` alone, and `MARKET` alone, where used in class 36/42 with
   financial meaning. **A `KUDI` mark in class 36 is the risk we care about most**
   — we have not been able to rule out an existing Kudi-branded fintech and need
   this checked directly.
5. **Logo/compound** — no composite mark is registered yet, but search for similar
   marks in case one is close enough to cause confusion

Also search:

- **NIPO accepted-but-pending applications**, not just registered marks. A pending
  application can block our filing later.
- **Recently published Nigerian Trademark Journal** entries, for anything similar
  in the pipeline.
