Your market. Your rate. — strongest objection

Reads as trading advice. "Know before the price moves" is a day-trader cliché, and this could be a charting product.

Why it survives: KudiMarket has no charts. We said outright we would not lean on charts. The only thing that moves, from a user's point of view, is their own money. In a market at dusk the question is not "will BTC move" — it is "did my cedi move before I knew?" That is the Patricia wound, in four words.

The decisive test — which ones work as product copy

A tagline that cannot also be a piece of product copy is decoration. If it works on a button and in the logo lockup, it is load-bearing.

Your market. Your rate. is four words, so it can be the escrow banner and the button on the escrow screen. Somebody's on the other end. and Nothing moves until you say so. are both too long for a button, so they are trapped on the marketing page.

decisive
Why this one. It is the only candidate that covers both products. For P2P it means you see the rate before you commit. For instant it means the instant quote is shown before money moves. Escrow, rate transparency, and the simplicity promise all fall out of the same four words — because they are the same promise: you are never surprised.
What it does not do, honestly. It carries no warmth. Somebody's on the other end. is friendlier and more distinctly human. If you think KudiMarket's real position is community rather than certainty, that is the one to take instead. This is a judgement about what leads — and certainty is what wins a deposit, warmth is what wins a return visit.
The test I will keep running. If we ever ship charts, Your market. Your rate. starts reading as trading advice and it should be re-examined. That is a real condition, not a hypothetical — the moment candles appear, the ambiguity becomes real. If charts are on the roadmap, take Somebody's on the other end. and a subtitle instead.
