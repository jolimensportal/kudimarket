What you get. The warmest option, and the only one grounded in the physical world the product is named after. Bronze feels like a market — hand-built, human, outdoors, alive. It also keeps a comfortable distance from gold, so it never looks like a cheaper Binance. The lightness drop from 0.813 to 0.424 is doing most of the work.
The cost
Bronze Light at 4.1:1 only clears AA at large text, so metadata needs Ink Mkt instead. And this is the option most at risk of drifting toward Binance gold if anyone brightens it for a "premium" tier.
The bank risk
None. Nearest green is 39.14 away, so it cannot read as a Ghanaian bank. Warmth here comes from pigment, not from institutional green.
The gold trap
Bronze and gold share a family. The discipline: never exceed lightness 0.46 on any core brand surface. Below that it is earth; above it, it becomes an exchange.
Red and green still hold

My recommendation
Choose this if the product leads with community and conversation. It is the most human of the three and the least corporate. It carries a slight risk of reading rustic rather than precise.
