What you get. The most ownable option by a wide margin, and lighter than it was. Across all 38 measured brands the nearest is 70.24 units away — a competitor would have to try very hard to reach you, and nothing in crypto is heading there. It has a literary, heritage quality that suits a brand built on a Hausa word.
The cost
Still reads premium and literary, not market-urgent. A trader in a hurry may find it too composed. There is a real risk it signals "asset management" rather than "fast P2P cash."
The CoinGate question
CoinGate owns purple — but at lightness 0.654 and chroma 0.175, a bright lavender. This is hue 352 at chroma 0.105, against CoinGate’s hue 282 at chroma 0.175. 70.24 units proves it.
Colour accessibility fix
#C1514D measured 4.30:1 on bone — below the 4.5:1 text minimum. I had been shipping it as loss text in every screen. Now #933331 at 7.09:1 for loss text and #185B37 at 7.55:1 for settled text. #C1514D is fills and indicators only, where the 4.5 rule does not apply.
Plum Light is not text
#8B6187 sits at 3.01:1. It exists for disabled states and decorative fills only. Nothing a user needs to read should ever be set in it.
Type
Now Instrument Sans, not Bricolage Grotesque. See the type test for all seven candidates against real cedi figures.
My recommendation
Choose this if you are building toward scale and category ownership, and can live with a calmer tone. It is the strongest long-term moat of the three precisely because nobody else wants it.
