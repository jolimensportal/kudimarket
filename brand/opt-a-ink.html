What you get. The strongest brand of the three and the least to defend. No hue can be challenged, imitated by a gradient, or confused with a bank. It reads as an instrument rather than an app — closer to a ledger than to a feed. The wordmark carries the entire identity, which means 1c's light/heavy bilingual split becomes the brand rather than a detail.
The cost
No warmth. Ink can read cold, austere, or fintech-luxury. If the product leans hard into community and conversation — seller photos, chat, trust — achromatic may undersell the human side.
The bank risk
None. Nearest green is 48.71 away. This is the only one of the three that cannot be mistaken for a Ghanaian bank.
The scaling risk
Ink has no headroom. There is nowhere darker to go, so hierarchy must be built from weight and space rather than shade. That is a discipline, and it is why the type does more work here than in B or C.
Red and green still hold
#C1514D loss and destructive only, #2F7D4F settled and escrow only, never a button. Both sit 37+ and 83+ units from Ink, so neither competes with the brand.
My recommendation
If KudiMarket's real position is trust through clarity, Ink is the honest expression of it. This is the one I would defend in a board meeting without flinching.
