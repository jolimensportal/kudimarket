But their warmth is their brand equity, and the warm-orange band is the single most crowded region in your market — Binance, Bybit and MTN GH all sit there, and AirtelTigo and UBA sit on the red side of it. One thing I will not do. Push the lightness until white text fails and then switch the button label to dark ink, which is what Claude does. It is legitimate for them because the terracotta is an accent, not a button.
