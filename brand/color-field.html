Why red
It is the one hue the category has abandoned. Every exchange reaches for gold, green, blue or purple because those read as finance. Deep oxblood reads as authority and permanence — lacquer, sealing wax, old ledger ink. Nobody builds a crypto brand on it.
Not "danger"
Oxblood is a pigment, not a warning. Danger is chrome red at high lightness — that is a different colour and this is not it. Depth is what separates them.
Ghana grounded
Deep red is ceremonial and authoritative across Ghanaian traditions — kente, Adinkra, chief’s regalia. Red-white-green is the flag. It reads as ours without borrowing American civic vocabulary.
Outlives the chain
No crypto vocabulary. No Binance gold to fight. Works for cedi, cedi or dollars because the hue is doing trust work, not tech work.
Accessibility
Verified against WCAG on bone #FCF6EE. Base 8.40:1, Deep 11.42:1, Market 5.99:1. White on Market 6.43:1. Every text pairing passes AA.
The risk
Red can read as loss to Western eyes, where red candles mean a falling price. Countered by pairing with bone rather than black — this never looks like a trading terminal. If you expand to US markets later, re-test.
